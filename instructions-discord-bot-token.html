How to Obtain & Submit Your Discord Bot Token

Follow these simple steps to set up your application on the Discord Developer Portal, configure required intents, and safely submit your bot credentials.

1

Create Your Application

Navigate to the official Discord Developer Portal and sign in with your Discord account.

  • Click the New Application button in the top right corner.
  • Type a name for your bot (e.g., MyServer Assistant) and accept the Developer Terms.
  • Click Create to open your new application settings.
  • Copy your Application ID from the General Information page—you will need this later!
2

Enable Privileged Gateway Intents

LuMiBot requires specific permissions to read command messages and manage server members accurately.

Crucial Step: If these options are not toggled ON, the bot will fail to process commands or respond inside your server.
  • On the left sidebar menu, click on the Bot tab.
  • Scroll down to the Privileged Gateway Intents section.
  • Toggle ON the following three switches:
    • • Presence Intent
    • • Server Members Intent
    • • Message Content Intent
  • Click Save Changes at the bottom of the screen.
3

Reset & Copy Your Bot Token

Your Bot Token serves as the secret access key for your bot application.

  • While still inside the Bot tab, locate the Build-A-Bot header.
  • Click the Reset Token button (you may be asked to enter a 2FA code).
  • Click Copy to copy the generated token string to your clipboard.
4

Secure Token Submission

Submit your bot details below to complete your setup integration.

Security Notice: Never share your personal Discord login password or Client Secret. Only provide the Bot Token generated in Step 3. All submissions are encrypted in transit.
