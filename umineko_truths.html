Umineko Truths

Overview

Bring the mystery and dramatic flair of Umineko: When They Cry into your Discord server! Activate truth modes to have your messages automatically styled in colored code blocks or gorgeous dialogue cards.

Available Commands

/color [mode] Activate a colored mode (Red, Blue, or Gold).
Everyone
/uoff Deactivate your current truth mode and return to normal chat.
Everyone

How it Works

Once you select a mode with /color, every message you send in that channel will be intercepted by LuMiBot and reposted as a formatted code block or dialogue card in your chosen truth color.
